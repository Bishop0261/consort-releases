Consort 0.8.0

  • Discreet mode: Consort can show who wrote and how many messages, never what they wrote. Press ⌃⌥D to hide all text at once.
  • Text, person by person: in Settings ▸ General choose whether message text is shown, and set it for each person too.
  • Easier to read: headings and titles are no longer cut off, in every language.
  • Send feedback with screenshots: cover private parts with black boxes, then send. It is saved and sent later if you are offline.
  • Choose who to reply to when several people are waiting: pick one person, or use a menu per person (Settings ▸ General).

The Terms of Use were updated: when you send feedback, Consort uploads what you wrote, your screenshots and, if you tick it, the logs.
